Reason about the trust boundary

    
A source audit establishes properties of the source you inspected—not automatically of the binary you execute.

    
Use the experiment to discover which other artifact must be trusted before source-level reasoning carries through to execution.

  






  
Chain of trust restored

  
You started from independently trusted compiler T, used it to build clean compiler.c, and then used that trusted compiler to build clean login.c. The hidden behavior disappeared.

  

    
Lesson 1 — Source ≠ binarySource review cannot by itself prove that the produced executable corresponds to that source.

    
Lesson 2 — Tools are in the trusted computing baseCompilers, build systems, dependencies, and other transformation tools can affect the final artifact.

    
Lesson 3 — Trust needs an anchorBreaking a self-reproducing compromise requires an independently trusted starting point or an independent verification technique.

  

  
Advanced connection: techniques such as reproducible builds and Diverse Double-Compiling can provide stronger evidence that a compiler binary corresponds to its source.
